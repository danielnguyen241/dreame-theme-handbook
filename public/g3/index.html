Frequently Asked Questions

Which suction mode should I use?

Eco suits light cleaning, Auto balances suction and battery life, and Turbo is for stubborn dirt.

When should I clean the cyclone and pre-filter?

Dreame recommends cleaning them at least once every 3–4 months.

How do I clean the roller brush?

Release the roller and remove tangled fibers. If you rinse it with water, leave it to air-dry for at least 24 hours before refitting.

Should I clean the dust cup?

Wipe it with a well-wrung cloth about once a month.

What should I avoid vacuuming?

Use the Z30 on dry surfaces. Avoid sharp objects and fine powders that can clump inside the vacuum.

310 AW and 150,000 RPM are maximum lab-test results. The 90-minute maximum applies in Eco mode under the stated test conditions. Filtration and coverage figures use Dreame’s test conditions. Actual results vary by surface, attachment and use.
