FAQ

How do the four L60 series models differ?

The L series includes 4 models: L60 Pro Ultra, our top-of-the-line model; L60 Ultra, which matches its slim, streamlined design; L60 Ultra PE, built for mid-range value; and L60 Ultra FE, our entry-level option. The main differences come down to navigation, obstacle avoidance, threshold crossing, suction power, battery life, and brush system.

How is the L60 Pro Ultra's mopping system?

Its mopping system applies 15N of downward pressure and rotates at 165RPM. Phase-change mop pads retain heat from 212°F (100°C) hot-water washing, helping loosen grease and stubborn stains while mopping.

How long can the L60 Pro Ultra run?

The L60 Pro Ultra's 6,400 mAh battery (vs. 5,200 mAh in other L60 models) delivers approximately 220 minutes of runtime, giving it the range to power ProLeap and cover larger homes. Actual runtime varies by settings and layout.
